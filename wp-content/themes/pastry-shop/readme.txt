== Description ==

The Pastry Shop Theme is a charming, feature-rich website template designed specifically for pastry shops, bakeries, dessert cafés, cake shops, and confectionery stores, frequently searched under terms like bakery eCommerce theme, cake shop online ordering template, dessert delivery website, pastry store layout, gourmet sweets shop design, and custom cake business website. Ideal for showcasing artisanal pastries, gourmet desserts, custom cakes, bakery products, and café menus, this theme provides business owners with a professional online presence that captures the essence of a local dessert shop. Built to highlight both sweet and savoury offerings, it’s perfect for cafés specialising in chocolate pastries, cupcakes, doughnuts, French pastries, or signature dessert boxes. Developed using the WooCommerce plugin for smooth online ordering and eCommerce functionality, it allows complete customization without any coding skills. Its clean, visually appealing design emphasizes high-quality product images with sections for displaying collections like croissants, cookies, tarts, muffins, and mini pastries for party platters. The theme includes flexible layouts to feature wedding cakes, bakery specialties, seasonal treats, and luxury desserts. Designed for a seamless shopping experience, it’s ideal for online bakery stores offering pastry delivery, dessert catering, and subscription boxes. Fully compatible with the WooCommerce Product Add-Ons plugin to support custom cake variations, personalised pastry requests, flavour selections, quantity customization, and special instructions, the theme enhances customer orders and conversions. With interactive menus, product galleries, sections for gluten-free and vegan options, and display areas for featured items, this theme is optimized for mobile responsiveness, dessert SEO, and online bakery marketing—making it a perfect choice for building a modern, visually delightful pastry brand.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your Pastry Shop right away.
